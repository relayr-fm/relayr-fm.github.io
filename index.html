/removeTake one song out of the queue

/diagnoseCheck what's wrong if audio sounds off

Questions, answered.

Does it actually play Spotify?

Paste any Spotify song, album or playlist link and Relayr plays it. Spotify doesn't let any bot stream its audio directly, so Relayr reads the track list from the link and finds each song on other services to play. It's how every Discord bot handles Spotify links.

What can I paste into /play?

A link to a song or playlist from Spotify, SoundCloud, YouTube or Bandcamp, a direct link to an audio file, or just a song name like "slow burn marlowe ave". It finds the best match.

How big a playlist can I queue?

Up to 500 songs at once. They're added to the queue straight away, so you're not waiting while it loads.

Why is there no volume command?

Discord already does it better. Right-click Relayr in the voice channel and drag the volume slider. It only changes the volume for you, so nobody else's listening is affected.

Will it stay in the channel forever?

No. Relayr leaves as soon as everyone else has left the channel, and after five minutes with nothing in the queue.

What permissions does it need?

Just the basics: connect and speak in voice channels, and send messages with embeds so it can show what's playing. Nothing that touches your server's settings or members.
